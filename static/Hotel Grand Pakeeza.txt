Hotel Grand Pakeeza
R2 Block Road 57 R2, Johar Town, Lahore, Johar Town, 54782 Lahore, Pakistan.
Located in Lahore, within 37 km of Wagah Border and 2.8 km of Emporium Mall, Hotel Grand Pakeeza provides accommodation with a terrace and free WiFi as well as free private parking for guests who drive. This 3-star hotel offers room service and a 24-hour front desk. Every room comes with a balcony with views of the city.
A continental breakfast is available daily at the accommodation.

Gaddafi Stadium is 11 km from Hotel Grand Pakeeza, while Packages Mall is 11 km away. Allama Iqbal International Airport is 17 km from the property
